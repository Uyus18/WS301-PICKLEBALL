Discover pickleball

Quick feet. Soft hands. One more point.

Pickleball turns a compact court into a fast game of reactions and angles. Serve diagonally, trade rallies with a perforated ball, and look for open space.

Play singles or doubles. The seven-foot non-volley zone, better known as the kitchen, rewards soft hands and smart placement at the net.
